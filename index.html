History

Est.
"A family without roots is like a tree without soil — it cannot stand the storms of time."

The Perayil family traces its origins to the lush waterways of Kuttanad, Kerala — a land of paddy fields below sea level, of serpent boat races and ancient churches. Generations of our family have called this unique landscape home.

Our family name, Perayil, carries within it the memory of a place, a house, and a people who have endured and flourished through centuries of Kerala's remarkable history. From the arrival of Saint Thomas the Apostle to the spice trade era, from colonial times to modern independence — our family has witnessed it all.

Today, members of the Perayil family are spread across India and the world, but the pull of the old homeland — its coconut groves, its backwater canals, its church bells on Sunday mornings — remains as strong as ever.


Our Village

Champakulam

Champakulam is a small island village in the Alappuzha district of Kerala, nestled in the heart of the Kuttanad backwaters. It is known for its ancient churches, its mango-lined waterways, and the famous Champakulam Moolam Boat Race — one of the oldest snake boat races in Kerala.

For the Perayil family, Champakulam is more than a location on a map. It is the soil from which we grew, the water that surrounded our childhood homes, and the community that shaped our values of faith, family, and fellowship.

About the Village

  • 📍 Alappuzha District, Kerala, India
  • 🚤 Home of the famous Champakulam Moolam Boat Race, one of Kerala's oldest
  • ⛪ Known for its centuries-old Syrian Christian churches and rich religious heritage
  • 🌾 Part of Kuttanad — the "Rice Bowl of Kerala," situated below sea level
  • 🌿 Surrounded by backwater canals, paddy fields and coconut groves
  • 🏠 The ancestral home of the Perayil Kudumbam for generations
